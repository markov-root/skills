# Revision method

Writing is rewriting, but a raw one-shot dumped as final is not revision. The
highest-leverage habit is that revision is **diagnosed and non-blind**: locate the
dominant defect, then make **one targeted change**. Intrinsic self-correction is
weak without an external oracle — a located defect plus a discriminating check
beats ritual iteration.

## Select by defect type (the provisional default)

Diagnose the single most consequential failure, then choose the matching move.
Higher-order concerns before lower-order ones — a broken argument is not worth
polishing:

- **Reconceive** — the controlling idea, framing, or structure is wrong. The
  reverse-outline can't recover a single governing point, or the point recovered
  isn't the one the brief wanted. Fix this first; line-editing a wrong structure
  is premature.
- **Substantiate** — a claim is unsupported, vague, or over-stated. Route through
  the specificity ladder ([`specificity.md`](specificity.md)); mark what you
  cannot source rather than inventing it.
- **Clarify** — the meaning is right but the reader won't get it. Apply
  reader-expectation mechanics: old/framing material in the **topic position**
  (sentence start), the most important new information in the **stress position**
  (sentence end), given-before-new chaining, subject–verb proximity, central
  actors as subjects and their actions as verbs (kills buried nominalizations —
  Lanham's Paramedic Method).
- **Polish** — voice, rhythm, concision, mechanics. Last. Cut before polishing;
  read aloud, because the ear catches false cadence and dead endings the eye
  approves.

Return **no more than three consequential findings, each anchored to a span**. A
review must add information or an oracle _not already in the draft_; "no material
defect found" is a valid, honest result.

## Ordered altitude passes — a heuristic, not a law

Within a single full edit, moving structure → paragraph → sentence → word →
mechanics is a useful ordering (fixing everything at once hides problems). But it
is a heuristic, **not** a mandatory five-pass pipeline: structure and sentences
interact, and a **holistic rewrite is allowed to beat a sequence of narrow
repairs** — it can resolve coupled problems and preserve rhythm together.

> **This default is provisional (ADR 0005), not doctrine.** Defect-driven revision
> is the current prior because its evidence base is stronger; the ordered-altitude
> approach is a defended alternative. The marginal-return evaluator (roadmap)
> adjudicates them empirically — including an ordered-altitude comparator and a
> voice/edge-retention regression axis — so present neither as settled.

## The deviation discipline

Genre bounds and these defaults are the floor, not a cage. To deviate — a
structure the genre doesn't expect, a kept "tell," a held edge — **make the piece
justify the different shape**: state, in one line, the effect the deviation buys
and why it beats the conventional move. An unjustified deviation is a defect; a
justified one is voice. Declared edges are review-only and survive the loop, but a
fact-or-coherence problem with a declared edge's _substance_ still blocks — the
edge ledger never shields a false claim.

## The recursive process

Prewrite → draft → revise → edit → proofread, looping. Separate generation from
editing when blocked (freewrite, "zero drafts", accept an ugly first draft), then
switch to disciplined revision. Reverse-outline before line-editing. The final
pass is genre-specific ([`genres.md`](genres.md)): docs need runnable steps,
journalism attribution, science limitations, marketing proof, fiction scene
function, social a hook. Provenance:
