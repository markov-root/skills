# Cliché / stock-phrase fixture

Unquoted filler fires: when it comes to planning, the team picked low-hanging fruit and called the tool a game changer.

The fact of the matter is that finding the bug was a needle in a haystack. First and foremost, this fix is a paradigm shift that will move the needle.

A quoted critic may write "when it comes to hype, this vendor pitch is a paradigm shift" without firing here.

> The memo said first and foremost, think outside the box, then circle back after the offsite.

After the blockquote, plain prose repeats last but not least and must stay audible.
